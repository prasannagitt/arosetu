# AroSetu

## 1. Project Overview

AroSetu is a full-stack healthcare coordination platform designed to
connect patients, doctors, laboratories, and administrators in one
centralized system.

## 2. Problem Statement

Healthcare information and activities are often scattered across
appointments, prescriptions, laboratory reports, and follow-up records.
Patients may have difficulty keeping track of their healthcare history,
while doctors and laboratories may lack a unified workflow for managing
patient information.

AroSetu aims to provide a centralized platform for managing these
healthcare interactions.
